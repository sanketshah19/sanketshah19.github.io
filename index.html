How I work

I like to understand the system before changing it.

I begin by clarifying the actual problem, the people affected by it, and the constraints surrounding it. I question assumptions, compare trade-offs, and consider what today’s decision could make easier—or harder—as the product grows.

I prefer solutions that address the underlying problem, remain understandable to the team, and improve the system beyond the immediate task.

My best work usually happens when the problem is still ambiguous and engineering, product, design, and business considerations need to be brought together.

AI-native, engineering-led

AI is part of my daily engineering system, not a substitute for it.

I use models and agents to accelerate research, exploration, implementation, review, debugging, and documentation. I still own the problem framing, architecture, trade-offs, verification, and the final decision to ship.

AI increases my throughput. Judgment sets the direction and the quality bar.

Beyond the interface

I follow the systems that shape technology and products: AI models and inference, software architecture, developer tools, startups, product strategy, Indian business, markets, economics, and geopolitics.

I prefer explanations that go beyond what happened and examine why it happened: how a technical system works, why a company makes a particular decision, what incentives shape a market, and what second-order effects might follow.

That breadth strengthens my product judgment. It helps me connect engineering choices with user behavior, operating constraints, and business outcomes.
